Come and See

When two men started following Jesus and asked where he was staying, he didn't give them an argument. He said, “Come and see” (John 1:39). This is ninety mornings of taking him up on it, one chapter at a time, starting with the Gospel that carries your name.

Yohann

Your name comes from Yohannes, the name John, from the Hebrew Yochanan: the Lord has been gracious. On Day 40 you'll read God using that same word to describe himself.

The morning

Same time every day, before the phone. Four steps, in this order.

  1. 1

    AskBefore you open it, pray one line: “Speak, Lord, your servant is listening” (1 Samuel 3:10).

  2. 2

    ReadOne chapter, once, slowly. A paper Bible if you have one. The phone will try to pull you somewhere else.

  3. 3

    WriteTwo lines in a paper notebook: what this shows me about God, and one thing I'll do today because of it.

  4. 4

    Pray it backTalk to God about what you read, in your own words. Then go do the one thing.

Why this order

Parts I to IV are mostly about who God is. The chapters aimed straight at work, confidence and money start in Part V. That's on purpose. In Scripture, people change after they meet God, not before. You don't have to fix yourself to start.

Before Day 1

Five rules for the road

Day numbers, not dates.
Miss a morning and you pick up at the next unread day. Never double up to catch up. Cramming works for exams. It doesn't work for knowing someone.
Read it before you ask about it.
Don't have AI summarize a chapter you haven't read. Questions afterward are good. Take them to a pastor, a study Bible, or someone reading with you.
The checkmarks are a bookmark.
They show where you are. They aren't a score, and there's no streak to protect.
Don't read alone.
Find a church this month, and one person who'll read along or check in once a week (Hebrews 10:24–25).
Do one thing.
Every morning ends with one concrete action. Reading without doing is exactly what James warns about (James 1:22).

Use a readable translation: NIV, ESV or CSB. If those feel heavy, try the NLT.

The journey

Nine parts, ninety days

Tap a day to mark it read; tap again to undo. Each part has one verse to learn by heart and one thing to write when you finish it.

Then

After Day 90

  • Read Luke, then Acts. Luke tells the story of Jesus in order; Acts tells what his followers did next.
  • Then read the whole Bible in a year, about three or four chapters a day. Pick a plan with your church so someone is reading alongside you.
  • Keep the morning, the notebook and the person you read with. Those three matter more than any plan, this one included.
  • Read your Day 1 notebook entry next to your Day 90 entry.
