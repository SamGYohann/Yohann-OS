Section II · Priority of claims

THE WATERFALL

Income enters at the top and falls through six tiers in strict order. A tier only fills once everything above it is satisfied. This is the whole budget — there is no separate list of rules to remember.

Monthly distribution—
Why this order

Obligations first because a missed rent payment is the only line item here that can end the plan outright. Reserve before career capital because an empty reserve turns every opportunity into a financing question. Career capital before retirement because you are sixteen months from a December 2027 graduation into a restructuring seat, and the return on a flight to an interview dwarfs the return on a marginal Roth dollar at your current balance. Risk capital last and capped, because your own trader self-assessment flagged overconfidence and revenge trading — the cap is the guardrail you built for yourself, compiled into the structure rather than left to willpower.

Section III · Downside

STRESS CASES

The base case is the one case that never matters. Each row re-runs the full thirteen-week bridge with one thing broken, and reports where the trough lands.

Shock parameters
Results—

A scenario fails when its trough drops below the covenant floor — one month of obligations. Failing the combined case is expected and not alarming. Failing a single-shock case means one ordinary bad week ends the plan.
